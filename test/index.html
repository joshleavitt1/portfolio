What changed

Bankers previously tabbed through 9+ tools to authenticate customers, understand relationships, and submit service requests—creating long silences and compliance risk. We replaced that maze with a single console that prioritizes clarity, guided flows, and safe automation.

Audience: Wealth & retail servicing
Role: Director of Product Design (IC + lead)
Timeframe: 14 weeks

My responsibilities

  • Partnered with Ops & Risk to define north star outcomes and KPIs.
  • Led discovery: diary studies, call-shadowing, and service blueprinting.
  • Established a compact design system and interaction patterns for rapid delivery.
  • Ran weekly playback with executives; aligned pilot scope with engineering.
  • Authored training narratives and measurement plan for rollout.
Challenges & constraints

The problem

  • Identity verification was manual and varied by line of business.
  • Customer context lived across CRM, core, ticketing, and knowledge base.
  • Service actions were free-form, creating policy drift and rework.
  • Onboarding new associates took 6–8 weeks before handling live calls.

Success criteria

  • Cut handle time by double digits without sacrificing compliance.
  • Reduce training time to under 3 weeks with guided playbooks.
  • Deliver a reusable UI stack that engineering can extend in-house.
  • Prove value via a 90-day pilot with telemetry for staffing decisions.
Research synthesis

Key insights

  • Bankers lose ~2.4 minutes per call revalidating identity across systems.
  • Confidence drops when they cannot see holdings, flags, and tasks together.
  • Supervisors lack real-time visibility into stuck calls and policy drift.
  • Most escalations stem from duplicate data entry and missed required steps.

Design principles

  • Make trust visible: lead with verification, risk, and sentiment signals.
  • Guide, don’t gate: allow autonomy while nudging policy adherence.
  • Stay in flow: keep primary actions within a single pane.
  • Explainable: every automated decision carries “why” and undo.

Opportunities

  • Unify ID verification across retail & wealth with configurable steps.
  • Auto-populate forms and tickets from CRM + core data to remove rekeying.
  • Embed expert playbooks for common requests (address change, wires, disputes).
  • Surface coaching signals to supervisors for live assist and staffing.
